Each portfolio reads the same profile schema, including skills, projects, photos, résumé, biography, contact links, education, experience and achievements. Their public page components and styles are different. Student authentication, access assignment, uploads, revisions and database storage are retained.

Open each website’s `/edit` route to update details. The Appearance tab changes the accent and introduction alignment within that website’s own design. The developer terminal accepts `projects`, `about`, `skills`, `journey`, `contact` and `help`; these commands only navigate the portfolio.

Project images and student photos are editable. Until students upload their own images, the websites show typographic project covers and initials. The bundled profiles contain sample details.
